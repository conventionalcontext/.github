# Getting help

**Questions, ideas, and problems:** open an issue in [conventionalcontext/conventionalcontext](https://github.com/conventionalcontext/conventionalcontext/issues). Search existing issues first.

**Security problems:** do not open an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Code of conduct concerns:** email conduct@conventionalcontext.org. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

**Anything else:** hello@conventionalcontext.org.

## What the maintainers support

- The `conventional-context` schema for OpenSpec, and the `docs/context/` templates.
- The latest `0.x` release.

## What they do not

- OpenSpec itself: ask in [OpenSpec's repository](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec).
- A specific AI agent or coding tool (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and others): ask its maintainers.
- Your project's own `docs/context/` content: it belongs to your project.

The maintainers are volunteers and answer when they can, usually within a week.
